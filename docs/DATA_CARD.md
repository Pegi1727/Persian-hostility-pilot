# Dataset card
**Task:** Human perception of policy stance, hostility, arousal, and naturalness in constructed Persian texts.

**Unit and coverage:** 32 stimuli, four scenarios, eight cells per scenario; three raters per stimulus; 96 rating records. Not a naturally sampled social-media corpus.

**Language:** Persian. Context and text are preserved as supplied; no tokenization/translation is applied.

**Labels:** Intended factorial labels reflect construction goals. Perceived labels and 1–7 ratings reflect annotator judgments. Neither is assumed to be an error-free diagnostic ground truth.

**Public files:** stimulus-level text/context and minimized record-level ratings. No candidate split or train/test designation applies to this pilot. Do not combine the pilot with candidate IDs or invent a train/test evaluation.

**Annotation provenance:** Owner states human ratings. Recruitment, background, training, consent/ethics, scale-anchor wording, and verified blinding require authentic documentation. These cannot be recovered from the rating values.

**Privacy:** Pseudonymous IDs retained; administrative and free-text annotator metadata omitted. Owner must still review disclosure risk and public-sharing authorization.

**Intended use:** Audit pilot manipulations, descriptive ratings, and agreement; reproduce reported statistics; develop future validation procedures.

**Out of scope:** Automated hate-speech performance claims, screening individuals, generalization to all Persian communication, final candidate-set validation, and treating emotional opposition as hostility by default.

**Rights:** No license asserted pending owner approval. See RIGHTS.md.
